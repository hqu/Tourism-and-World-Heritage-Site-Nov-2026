About this data

World Bank, World Development Indicators: International tourism, number of arrivals (ST.INT.ARVL), last updated 8 October 2026. Arrivals count overnight visitors who travel to a country other than their usual residence. Original figures come from UN Tourism's Yearbook of Tourism Statistics.

Regions are the World Bank's seven geographic groups. Middle East & North Africa has no regional total for 1995, so it is missing from the 1995–2019 view. Countries need a figure in both the start year and 2019 to be ranked. 2020 is shown in the comparison chart but left out of growth rates: the pandemic cut arrivals sharply and only 132 places reported that year.

World Heritage sites come from the UNESCO World Heritage List export (whc001), 1,273 properties inscribed from 1978 to 2026, with map positions from its coordinates. A site shared by several countries counts once for each. Comparisons with 2019 arrivals use only sites inscribed by 2019. South Sudan, North Korea, the Holy See and Afghanistan have sites but no arrivals figure, so they are not in the comparisons. Correlation shows that two things move together. It does not show that sites cause tourism: large, well-connected countries tend to have both.
